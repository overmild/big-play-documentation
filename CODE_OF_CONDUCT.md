# Code of Conduct

Big Play brings together people with very different backgrounds and technical experience. To keep this a place people want to keep contributing to:

- **Be kind and patient**, especially with people who are newer to a tool, a topic, or the project itself. There are no "dumb" questions here.
- **Assume good intent**, but address impact — if something someone said or did caused harm, it's fine to say so plainly and kindly.
- **Disagree on ideas, not people.** Critique the proposal, not the proposer.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks** of any kind, in any doc, issue, PR, or discussion.
- **Give credit.** If you build on someone else's proposal or idea, say so.

## If something goes wrong

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this, reach out to a maintainer listed in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) directly. Reports will be handled discreetly and taken seriously.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, comments, or issues that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and in serious or repeated cases may restrict someone's participation in the project.
